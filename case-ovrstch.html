The problem

It started as merch for a YouTube channel. I wanted it to become a real label with real, physical product.

  • Merch, not a brand.Print-on-demand merch supporting a channel, with no identity of its own.
  • A luxury standard.The goal was physical product made to a high-end standard, not just printed shirts.
  • One operator.Design, sourcing, production, QA, logistics and support all had to run at once, lean and hands-on.
