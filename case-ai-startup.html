The problem

Teams were fine-tuning AI models on customers’ own data to cut hallucinations, and the work was as uncertain as the research behind it.

  • Three model families.Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude, all reached through their APIs and tuned on proprietary customer datasets.
  • Ambiguous needs.Research and product needs were loosely defined, and priorities moved.
  • Very different people.Data scientists, product owners, engineers, directors and the C-suite all had to stay in sequence.
  • Slow pipelines.Data processing and review were the bottleneck between an idea and a tuned model.

How I ran it

Agile for work that is half research.

Open one to see why

Ambiguity becomes a KPI.Show why

A research goal nobody can measure cannot be planned, so each became a structured backlog with measurable KPIs.

Two ways of working, one set of norms.Show why

Some workstreams ran Scrum and others Kanban. All of them shared one ceremony cadence, one set of communication protocols and one escalation path.

Surface problems daily.Show why

The war room existed so issues showed up the same day, not at the sprint review.

Fix the slowest step first.Show why

Throughput was set by processing and review, so that is where the re-sequencing went.

Report before you are asked.Show why

Senior stakeholders got proactive reporting, including where scope was likely to grow next.
