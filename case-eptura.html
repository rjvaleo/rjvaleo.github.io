The problem

About ten teams across India and California, in a matrixed enterprise, with delivery that had to be transparent and audit-ready.

  • Ten teams, two time zones.Offshore teams in India and onshore teams in California, with dependencies running between them.
  • Governance on top.Program-increment cadences in a matrixed organization, where delivery had to stay audit-ready.
  • New to Agile.Marketing, UX and data teams were working reactively, outside any Agile rhythm.
  • Work spilled over.Churn and carryover from sprint to sprint made iterations hard to predict.
  • Blockers above the teams.Some impediments were structural, and no single team could clear them on its own.

How I ran it

The principles behind the numbers.

Open one to see why

Readiness before commitment.Show why

Stories passed a readiness check before a team committed to them: clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done. That is what cut the carryover.

Limit work in progress.Show why

WIP discipline kept teams finishing work instead of starting it, so iterations became predictable.

Every retro ends in a change.Show why

A retro that does not change the workflow is a meeting. Each one had to produce a measurable improvement.

Fix the structure, not the team.Show why

When a blocker sat above the teams, I took it to leadership, HR and peer coaches instead of asking the teams to work around it.

One way to see the work.Show why

Standard Jira workflows, fields and dashboards meant leadership saw every team the same way.
