I decide what to build, then I get it built.

I started as a front-end developer in 2000, became a producer, then a delivery lead. For twenty years I turned what clients wanted into what teams shipped. AI agents closed the last gap: now I can build what I specify.

By day I run a ten-team release train on a tolling program and co-build the requirements app it plans from. On my own time I have shipped five music products with AI agents. Before that: voice production for Meta’s AI speech platform and the Ray-Ban Stories launch, two streaming apps on five platforms at A+E Networks, an agile PMO at Eptura, and a decade of agency production for American Express, Citi, Toyota and Unilever. I make music as Isomer Transition, which is why the products I build for myself are for musicians.
