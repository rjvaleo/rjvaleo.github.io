Tolling modernization program

A large tolling modernization program with fixed, contracted scope and a launch date that does not move. I run the release train, and I built the tools it plans from.

The problem

  • Ten workstreams, each staffed by its own vendor or contractor team.
  • Teams onshore, offshore and in Europe.
  • Scope is fixed. The only levers are sequencing and added resources.

What I did

  • Own release train planning across five 10-week increments.
  • Interview every workstream lead before planning.
  • Rebuilt the cross-workstream Scrum of Scrums.
  • Ran the two-day increment close-out roadshow for the client.
  • Run a retrospective with all leads at the end of each increment.
  • Built the planning board, and co-built the requirements app the program now plans from.

What changed

  • Program Increment 1 closed with all 74 committed requirements accepted.
  • 218 test scenarios cover every testable requirement.
  • Every lead plans from one script and one picture.
  • The delivery report and the close-out roadshow came from the board.

Decisions

  • Interview every lead before planning.Ten vendors plan in ten ways. The interviews produced one script that every lead now plans from.
  • Sequence the work; don't cut it.Scope is fixed by contract. The levers are order and added people, so the board is built to show carry-forward early.
  • Build the tool when the tool can't be bought.Jira Standard and no admin rights ruled out the usual add-ons. I prototyped the board myself and wrote it as a spec the Jira owner could build from.

How AI was used

  • The planning board and the requirements app were both built with AI agents, test-first.
  • Reusable AI skills do the recurring work: a readout from a meeting transcript, and a refresh of the roadshow numbers from delivery exports, reconciling increment figures against program-wide ones.
  • I am the program's AI resource for the business analyst team.
