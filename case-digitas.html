DigitasLBi was SunTrust's agency of record. I was one of seven people on site at the bank every day, running two teams in a SAFe program.

The problem

Two teams in a SAFe program at a regulated bank, with development and QA split across two countries.

  • Regulated by default.A SOX-compliant financial-services program, where reporting had to be disciplined and audit-ready.
  • Split across borders.Onshore teams in the US, with development and QA offshore in Costa Rica.
  • Program-level commitments.Both teams had to deliver against PI objectives, in step with the rest of the release train.
  • Inside the client.One of seven agency people working on site at the bank every day.

The program increment

Every increment ran the same loop, for both teams.

Select a step in the loop

PI objectives

Both teams committed to program-increment objectives, and I tracked progress against them.

Iteration planning

Requirements fleshed out with the product owners and turned into backlog stories, with goals aligned to the product features.

Build and test

Development and QA split between the US and Costa Rica, with every ceremony run so both sides could work as one team.

Dependencies

PI-aligned dependencies, release timing and delivery risk, coordinated across the program and escalated when blocked.

Reporting

Velocity and resource use tracked in HP Agile Tracker, team dashboards maintained, and program leadership trained in Rally.

Improve

Process efficiencies found and agreed with stakeholders before any major change, plus coaching to strengthen how the teams applied SAFe.
